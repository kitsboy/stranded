# Stranded Value — Institutional & Partner Ask Sheet

Use this structured brief in conversations with infrastructure funds, sovereign/pension desks, First Nations development corporations, energy operators, and EU consortium partners.

## 1. Who We Are Partnering With
1. **Institutional & Sovereign Infrastructure Allocators (C$5M – C$2.5B+ tranches):** Project-finance debt and equity partners for multi-site generator + capture-hut rollouts under the Two-SPV structure.
2. **Oil, Gas, Coal & Municipal Landfill Operators:** Facility owners across our 2,611 mapped ECCC sites seeking zero-CapEx or JV flare/vent monetization and continuous methane MRV.
3. **First Nations & Indigenous Economic Development Corporations:** Co-owners leveraging AIOC (C$3B guarantee), CIB Indigenous Clean Power, and FNCEBF equity pathways across Treaty 6, 7, 8, BC, SK, and ON territories.
4. **Hardware, Genset & Capture Hut OEMs:** Global fabricators of CSA/CE/UL-rated 10ft/20ft/40ft ASIC enclosures, H₂S/siloxane scrubbers, and Jenbacher/CAT/MAN/Cummins gas engines.
5. **EU Horizon Cluster 5 & EUREKA/Eurostars Consortium Partners:** European RTOs (TNO, Fraunhofer, SINTEF) and cleantech SMEs co-applying under Canada's Horizon Europe association.

## 2. What Stranded Value Brings to the Table
- **Complete 2,611-Site Enriched Canadian Intelligence Layer:** Instant screening of reported CH₄ flow, Stranded Score™ v3, generator sizing, and peer cohorts.
- **Turnkey Engineering & Financial Transparency:** Side-by-side Live Network vs. Optimistic hashprice modeling, H₂S/seasonal derates, gas-decline curves, and instant Bank Pack exports (MD, CSV, TSV, HTML, PDF, JSON).
- **Cryptographic Auditability:** Dataset integrity and proof-of-burn / proof-of-giving rails anchored to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps (`satohash.io`).
- **The 10% Sovereign Pension Policy Framework:** A clear, non-partisan policy blueprint designed to align citizens, First Nations, provinces, and institutional capital.

## 3. What We Explicitly Do NOT Claim (Full Honesty Doctrine)
- We never claim "guaranteed returns" or hide network difficulty/halving risk.
- We never claim a landfill or wellhead vents 100% of its gas unless the federal filing publishes a fugitive/vent split.
- We never claim ASIC compute servers qualify directly for clean-tech tax credits — we separate upstream abatement/power generation (SPV 1) from compute (SPV 2).
